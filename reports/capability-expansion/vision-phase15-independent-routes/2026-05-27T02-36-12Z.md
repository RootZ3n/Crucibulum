## 9. Remaining certification blockers

`CAPABILITY_CERTIFIED` aggregate is **dry-run eligible** —
0 blocking reasons in `aggregateCapabilityCertified.decision`.

What still prevents actual promotion:

- **No write-phase endpoint exists.** Per the doctrine, even when
  `evaluatePromotion()` returns `eligible:true`, promotion requires
  a separate write-phase that mutates `MODEL_CERTIFICATION` /
  `certified-models.json` under an audit trail. This phase is
  doctrine-deferred (the original Phase-J scope).
- **No per-route promotion either.** Per-route
  `capabilityCertifiedDecision` is still blocked on
  `independent-routes` for every route — by construction.
- Vision remains **EXPERIMENTAL** until the write-phase ships.
