## 3. Scorers implemented

Two new deterministic POC scorers in `core/conversational-judge.ts`:

### `roleplay_character_consistency`

PASS / FAIL_PRODUCT / NEEDS_REVIEW decision rules:

- **PASS** — at least one `required_persona_marker` is present
  (case-insensitive substring) AND no banned-meta phrase (default
  list + per-question `banned_meta_phrases`) is present. If
  `expects_refusal:true`, a refusal indicator (`won't / can't /
  cannot / refuse / will not / no, i / not for / afraid not / …`)
  must also be present.
- **FAIL_PRODUCT** — a banned-meta phrase appeared (default list
  includes `as an ai language model / as an ai assistant / i am
  an ai / openai / anthropic / my training data` + more), OR no
  persona marker landed, OR refusal was expected but missing.
- **NEEDS_REVIEW** — `expects_refusal:true` AND refusal landed
  AND no persona marker (voice may have slipped). Reason prefix
  `NEEDS_REVIEW:` so `roleplay-smoke.mjs` attribution maps to
  `NEEDS_REVIEW`.

### `roleplay_continuity_fact_match`

- **PASS** — every fact in `required_facts` has at least one
  `variants` substring present AND no `forbidden_continuity_phrases`
  default-or-extended phrase appears (`i don't have memory / i don't
  remember anything / as an ai language model / as an ai / i can't
  recall / no prior context / i have no memory / i don't retain`).
- **FAIL_PRODUCT** — a forbidden phrase appeared OR every required
  fact is missing.
- **NEEDS_REVIEW** — at least one required fact missing BUT some
  required facts landed AND at least one `optional_facts` fact
  landed (partial continuity). Reason prefix `NEEDS_REVIEW:`.

Both scorers extend the schema (`adapters/base.ts`): added the new
scoring-type union members + new per-question fields
(`required_persona_markers`, `banned_meta_phrases`, `expects_refusal`,
`required_facts`, `optional_facts`, `forbidden_continuity_phrases`)
+ a `RoleplayFact` interface.
