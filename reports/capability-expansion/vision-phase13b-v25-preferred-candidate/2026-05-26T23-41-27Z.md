## 4. Why v2.5 is preferred (rationale)

From Phase 13-A live evidence (see commit `8268488` and the
Phase 13-A report
`reports/capability-expansion/vision-phase13a-mimo-v25-replacement/`):

| Metric | xiaomi/mimo-v2.5 (Phase 13-A) | xiaomi/mimo-v2-omni (Phase 12) | gpt-5.4-mini (Phase 12) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Smoke (5 tests) | 5 / 5 PASS @ $0.0003 | 5 / 5 PASS @ ~$0.0012 | 4 / 5 PASS @ $0.0003 |
| Stability (15 cells) | 15 / 15 STABLE_PASS @ $0.0004 | 15 / 15 STABLE_PASS @ $0.0036 | 12 / 15 STABLE_PASS @ $0.0008 |
| Recurring attributions | none | none (after Phase 12) | MODEL on object-count |
| Cost per 5-test run | ~$0.000169 | ~$0.001200 | ~$0.000267 |
| Cost per 10k runs (est.) | ~$1.69 | ~$12.00 | ~$2.67 |
| Required scorer calibration | none | yes (Phase 12 max_chars contract change) | none |
| Verbose-chain-of-thought uncertainty quirk | no | yes | no |

v2.5 matches v2-omni on stability while being **~9× cheaper per
stability cell** and **not requiring any scorer-side
accommodation**. v2.5 also passes `vision-object-count-001`, where
gpt-5.4-mini consistently fails (MODEL miscount). Across all
three tested routes, v2.5 is the only one that combines 100%
stable-pass with sub-$2 / 10k runs.

Public-pricing context (consistent with the operator-supplied
external evidence): OpenRouter lists `xiaomi/mimo-v2.5` at
materially lower per-token pricing than `xiaomi/mimo-v2-omni`,
especially on output tokens. Crucible's Phase 13-A spend per
stability cell is consistent with this delta.

## 5. Why Omni remains the fallback (rationale)

Even though v2.5 is the preferred candidate by every Phase 13-A
metric, **Omni is not deprecated or removed in this phase**:

- v2-omni has more historical evidence on Crucible (Phase 4,
  Phase 7, Phase 10, Phase 12 baselines). It is the path-proven
  route through the OpenRouter `openai_image_url` transport for
  this codebase.
- A single phase's evidence on v2.5 (5/5 smoke + 15/15 stability)
  is sufficient for a *candidate* verdict, not for default
  immutability. If the provider regresses on v2.5 (price spike,
  quality regression, transport breakage), operators need an
  immediate fallback that has already been proven on Crucible's
  fixtures.
- The capability doctrine
  (`docs/CAPABILITY_CERTIFICATION_DOCTRINE.md`) requires multiple
  routes for CAPABILITY_CERTIFIED; keeping Omni in the registry
  preserves that route diversity even though Phase 13-B does not
  promote anything.
- Removing the route would mean rewriting historical reports'
  cross-references and breaking the Phase B / 12 evidence trail.

Operationally: Omni stays in `MODEL_CERTIFICATION.models[]` at its
prior `PROVIDER_TESTED` main-suite tier (untouched), in
`VISION_TESTED_ROUTES` with a legacy-fallback `phase` label, in
`VISION_PROVEN_ROUTE` as the historical constant, and in
`VISION_ROUTE_RECOMMENDATIONS.legacyFallbacks[]` as the explicit
fallback recommendation. All four surfaces describe it as the
legacy / proven fallback. None promote it back to preferred.
