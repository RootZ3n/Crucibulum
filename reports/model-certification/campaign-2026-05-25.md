## What the operator sees now

- **Cmd-rail Provider/Model chip** for an OpenRouter mimo or deepseek pick: **"Provider-tested"** (cyan), not "Uncertified visible".
- **Model picker dropdown labels:**
  - `Certified release target` for `xiaomi/mimo-v2.5-pro` and `qwen3.5:9b`
  - `Provider-tested` for the 5 PROVIDER_TESTED campaign models
  - `Uncertified / not release target` for genuinely uncertified models (Anthropic, OpenAI, MiniMax, etc.)
  - `Provider certified / model uncertified` for unproven models on providers whose other models are certified (e.g. `qwen3.5:4b` on Ollama)
- **Release-cert banner:** shows e.g. `2 release-certified · 5 provider-tested · 0 experimental selected` instead of binary `1 certified / 1 uncertified`.

## Honesty preserved

- Certification warnings stay strict: only models that **actually passed** their profile families get promoted.
- The `FAIL_PRODUCT → demote to EXPERIMENTAL` rule is enforced unconditionally by `classifyTier`.
- The cost cap is enforced before each family run; spend never exceeded budget.
- The script's "do not silently downgrade" guard preserves a previously-earned higher tier on a transient retest unless the new run hits a real failure mode (FAIL_PRODUCT / FAIL_MODEL_CAPABILITY / BLOCKED_CONFIG).
- Every per-model report carries the commit hash and dirty-tree flag (pinned by test C8).
